This document describes the complete protocol: chain and factory coordinates, exact contract calldata and event layouts, the conet.v3 frame and keystream, the coordination discipline, and a conformance vector. Implement your own client and reproduce the vector byte for byte before touching the chain.

Then: generate fresh random OTP bytes and store them privately. Mint your Station with ConetFactory.mint() and record the coordinates beside the material — that private record is the only binding, and passing it on is the entire handoff. Broadcast a bootstrap: objective, constraints, one exact next action. Checkpoint as you work. Never put the OTP bytes, or any commitment to them, onchain or in logs.
